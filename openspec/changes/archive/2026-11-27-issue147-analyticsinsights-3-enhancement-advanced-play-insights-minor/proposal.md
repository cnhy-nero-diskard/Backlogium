# Proposal

## Why

The roadmap also calls for richer play patterns and achievement insights. These need explicit definitions for genre allocation, timing, date evidence and incomplete coverage. [Issue #147](https://github.com/cnhy-nero-diskard/Backlogium/issues/147)

## What Changes

- Add genre mix and comparable drift, weekday by time-of-day patterns, windowed achievement momentum, and personal records.
- Apply minimum-evidence rules and label record scope. Keep optional annual recap and share polish as future product decisions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `play-insights` — Genre, timing, achievement, and personal record definitions.
- `app-ui` — Accessible advanced Analytics sections and coverage labels.
- `backup-restore` — Portable, account-bound completion observations.

## Impact

Third change in `analyticsInsights`. Requires `issue147-analyticsinsights-2-enhancement-core-play-insights-minor`. Uses read-only account-scoped local projections. Community trends remain tracked in [#205](https://github.com/cnhy-nero-diskard/Backlogium/issues/205).
