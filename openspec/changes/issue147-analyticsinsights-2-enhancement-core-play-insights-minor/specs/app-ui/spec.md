# App UI Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Analytics offers accessible core play insights
Analytics SHALL present compact comparisons and core insights for play mix, variety, sessions, Focus and quest consistency. It SHALL provide an activity calendar with textual date details and reuse the existing inspected day interaction.

#### Scenario: Long selected range
- **WHEN** the player selects 90 days or a year
- **THEN** the primary trend uses weekly or monthly aggregates and the calendar still supports inspecting an exact local date and its game breakdown

#### Scenario: Alternative to precise touch
- **WHEN** the player navigates with accessibility actions or enlarged text
- **THEN** period and selected-day labels, values and interactions remain reachable without relying on color or chart geometry
